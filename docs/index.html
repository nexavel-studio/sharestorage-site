Getting started

Up and running
in three steps.

No cloud accounts, no router setup. Just two phones on the same WiFi.

01

Set up the vault phone

Install ShareStorage on the old phone, choose Vault mode, pick a storage location, and set a space limit.

02

Add people & share the QR

Tap "Add Person" for each family member, then show them the connection QR from the vault home screen.

03

Connect & sync

Install on the everyday phone, scan the QR, set your password, and tap "Sync now" to back up your photos.
